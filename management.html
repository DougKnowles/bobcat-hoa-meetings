How to read it

  • Roles are as the minutes state them. AMI managers are titled CAM or LCAM; Sentry minutes list "Brittany Polston, Sentry Management", and her CAM title comes from the Jan 21, 2025 minutes. Gabriella Wilson's and Joline Williams' roles are not stated in the minutes.
  • Annual members' meetings and workshops are not columns; where the manager chaired or took minutes at the annual meeting held the same evening as an organizational meeting, that is in the notes.
  • Recording-secretary duty: AMI's minutes (2017 to 2024) were usually "respectfully submitted for the Secretary by" the AMI manager.
