"Attended" counts the meetings shown above while the person was on the Board (a meeting where someone was appointed in absentia is not counted). Service before Jan 2017 or at meetings without minutes is not shown.

How to read it

  • Offices are as recorded at the start of each meeting (roll call or statement of quorum). Changes voted during a meeting are in the notes. Organizational meetings (held right after the annual members' meeting) show the officers elected there.
  • "Not present" means the minutes say the director was absent or do not list them among those present.
  • "Attended remotely" covers phone, Skype, Zoom and Teams. When a whole meeting was held by Zoom, everyone is marked remote.
  • Annual members' meetings and workshops are not shown; they don't record which directors attended.
